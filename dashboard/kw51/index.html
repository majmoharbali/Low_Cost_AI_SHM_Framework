Sources and drawings

The plan is cropped from the supplied overview.pdf. The marker registration uses the bearing centres and the longitudinal positions in select_sensors.m and model_kw51.m. Positions are for replay interpretation.

Change flags come from the pilot’s separately calibrated layout scores. The reference test, retrofit and post-retrofit periods each retain scheduled hours where the analysis abstained. A change flag indicates a frequency deviation under the pilot rule.
