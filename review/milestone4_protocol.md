# KW51 sensor reduction and measurement degradation pilot

Use scheduled ambient vibration recordings from the [official KW51 v1.0 record](https://zenodo.org/records/3745914). The source data are synchronous, provider-preprocessed acceleration time series. The published hourly SSI results supply reference frequencies for choosing FDD search bands and checking agreement during development. They do not supply the reduced-channel estimates. Use the six common deck channels, which provide both lateral and vertical directions. Exclude the arch channels because their availability changed around the intervention. [Source description](https://bwk.kuleuven.be/bwm/kw51) · [data paper](https://ascelibrary.com/doi/10.1061/%28ASCE%29BE.1943-5592.0001668).

## Prespecified recordings and verification

- Select the earliest recording in each scheduled UTC hour. Use 00:00 and 12:00 on 1–10 April 2019 for development and 11–20 April for calibration. Hold out 00:00 on 21–30 April. Use 00:00 on days 1, 3, 5, 7, 9 and 11 of July 2019 and January 2020 as separate retrofit and post-retrofit samples. The 62 planned acquisitions are sparse and represent a bounded pilot. Report every absent or unusable acquisition.
- Verify download integrity against each member's ZIP CRC, record its SHA-256 and retain the original files. Verify the recorded time vector, sample rate and shared rows with the published units and channel labels. The reader must reject malformed data and retain unavailable channels explicitly.
- Apply a common polyphase anti-alias resampling by 16, then use the same roughly 120-second interval for all cases. Use the foundation's Hann-Welch FDD with 2,048 samples per segment and 50% overlap. Keep its default prominence, competing-peak and power checks. All comparisons use the clean simultaneous full six-channel FDD as a practical reference.
- For each Milestone 3 mode (3, 5, 6, 9 and 13), use the median provider SSI estimate in at least eight paired development hours as the centre of a fixed ±0.12 Hz search band. A mode must be identified by full-deck FDD in at least 75% of usable development records, with at least eight paired estimates and agreement within two FDD bins on at least 70% of those pairs. Frequency agreement with the published hourly SSI is a comparability check given the same nominal hour. Preserve coverage for all candidate modes. Use up to two qualifying modes for fixed-panel change scoring, ranking by full-deck development identification count and then ascending mode ID. If none qualifies, the scoring result remains unavailable.

## Layouts, corruptions and scoring

- Test the clean six-channel reference, engineered four- and two-channel layouts, and three distinct seeded random layouts per size (seed 47). Choose the engineered subsets using only development FDD shapes from the qualifying modes. Require both measurement directions. Rank candidates by their worst retained fraction of median mode-shape energy, then position coverage, then channel index. If no modes qualify for this selection, require both directions and use position coverage as the criterion.
- Inject four independent scenarios into signal copies at the study sample rate before Welch/FDD. Apply each to both the six-channel layout and the engineered two-channel layout. Add independent Gaussian noise with RMS set to 5 dB below total recorded RMS per channel. Add a DC bias of +0.002 and −0.002 m/s² on alternating channels. Apply channel delays of 0–3 study-rate samples with a common crop and no circular wrap. Drop a two-second burst on one channel every fourth scheduled acquisition. Keep original measurement noise in all cases. DC bias provides a control because Welch removes the segment mean.
- For each clean layout, learn frequency medians and scaled MADs on complete development rows, with a one-bin scale floor. Calibrate the maximum absolute standardised deviation on a separate clean calibration sample using the 99th percentile (higher order statistic). Require at least eight complete acquisitions in each block. Freeze that layout's model and cutoff for its clean and degraded evaluations. This small calibration sample gives at most twenty tail observations and the cutoff typically equals the sample maximum. Treat flags as a descriptive indicator. Hourly persistence cannot be estimated from these sparse recordings.
- Evaluate each layout on identical held-out acquisitions. Report availability and reasons, lost modes compared with clean full-deck FDD, frequency disagreement and shape agreement on shared channels conditional on a valid pair, and per-window identification runtime. Report score/flag availability and disagreements on shared scorable acquisitions by period. Report guards and unavailable scenarios as findings. Phase, temperature, excitation, and upstream identification conditions differ, so phase differences do not isolate retrofit effects.
